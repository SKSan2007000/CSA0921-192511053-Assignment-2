Task 6 – Hospital Emergency Triage

Complexity:
• PriorityQueue offer/add: O(log n).
• PriorityQueue poll: O(log n).
• PriorityQueue peek: O(1).
• Storage: O(n).

Decision: A normal FIFO queue cannot prioritise critical patients. PriorityQueue orders patients by severity and then arrival time. Repeated poll() calls produce the treatment order; direct iteration over the queue is not guaranteed to be sorted. Waiting-time escalation should be defined and approved by clinical staff to reduce starvation.
