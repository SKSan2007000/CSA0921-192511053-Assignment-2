Task 7 – Ticket Generation Performance Redesign

Complexity:
• Repeated immutable String concatenation may approach O(k²) work as the accumulated text is copied repeatedly.
• StringBuilder appends are amortised O(1) each, so constructing the final output is O(L) in the output length.
• The builder requires O(L) storage for the resulting text; pre-sizing can reduce buffer expansions.

Decision: Use a pre-sized StringBuilder for repeated ticket construction. The measured speed-up applies to this workload and machine; it should not be treated as a universal performance guarantee.
