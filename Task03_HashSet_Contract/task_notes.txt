Task 3 – Student De-duplication with HashSet

Complexity:
• HashSet add, contains and remove: O(1) average; O(n) in the worst case when many collisions occur.
• Computing the hash and comparing an ID take O(m), where m is the identifier length.
• TreeSet add, contains and remove: O(log n), while maintaining sorted order.

Decision: Override equals() and hashCode() consistently. The identity field is final so that its hash value cannot change after insertion. Equal objects must have equal hash codes.
